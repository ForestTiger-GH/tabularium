# Financial Reporting

Primary machine-readable financial statements and closely related reporting artifacts published by Russian entities and groups, plus documented access bridges to canonical Bank of Russia regulatory reporting for banks.

## Route

- [ifrs/](ifrs/) — IFRS reporting.
- [ras/](ras/) — Russian Accounting Standards reporting artifacts.
- [ras-banks/](ras-banks/) — remote-source bridges to Bank of Russia regulatory forms for credit institutions.
