# SPB Exchange

Machine-readable materials documenting recurring SPB Exchange publications.

## Route

- [`trading-results/`](trading-results/) — recurring trading-results materials, including monthly and annual summaries.

## Provenance

Official SPB Exchange publications are the preferred primary source. For some historical periods, the original official pages are no longer publicly available and the surviving record exists only through third-party publications that reproduce or report figures originally released by SPB Exchange.

Such third-party files are retained only as historical preservation proxies. They are not equivalent to the unavailable primary-source originals and must retain their actual publisher and provenance.
