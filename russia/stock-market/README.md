# Stock Market

Primary machine-readable publications from Russian stock exchanges.

## Route

- [`moex-exchange/`](moex-exchange/) — recurring publications from Moscow Exchange.
- [`spb-exchange/`](spb-exchange/) — recurring publications from SPB Exchange.

## Publication series

| Exchange | Series | Frequency | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moscow Exchange | Retail investor activity | Monthly; annual summary releases may also occur | Official Moscow Exchange website |
| Moscow Exchange | Index reviews | Quarterly | Official Moscow Exchange website |
| Moscow Exchange | Bond secondary trading | Monthly | Official Moscow Exchange website |
| SPB Exchange | Trading results | Monthly | Official SPB Exchange website |
