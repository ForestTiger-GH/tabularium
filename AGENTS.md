# AGENTS.md

## Repository purpose

tabularium is a public, source-faithful corpus of machine-readable primary publications, selected source-extracted economic observations, and documented bridges to canonical high-volume primary datasets.

## Core rules

1. Store primary public publications and narrowly scoped source-faithful observations extracted from them only. A narrowly documented historical preservation proxy may be retained when the original primary publication is no longer publicly accessible. Preserve the proxy's actual publisher and provenance, and never represent it as the unavailable primary-source original.
2. A high-volume or frequently updated official primary dataset may be represented by a documented remote-source bridge instead of a mirrored artifact when the canonical publisher provides stable public access. A bridge must identify the canonical source, document retrieval and provenance rules, preserve source semantics, and must not present derived or analytical values as source data.
3. Preserve source meaning and reported values. Do not add analysis, interpretation, normalization, derived calculations, or private/internal data.
4. The authoritative source corpus lives under `scrolls/`. Route by country first unless an explicitly documented cross-country or source-institution route applies. `scrolls/world/` contains the documented non-Russian and international routes. Non-Russian corporate publications belong under `scrolls/world/corporate-disclosures/`; preserve the reporting-entity jurisdiction there with an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 prefix in artifact names and registry metadata. Stable public-authority and survey source-institution routes live under the documented `authorities/` and `surveys/` containers; within those containers, the publisher or institution may be the durable routing axis. Within each route, use stable source classes documented by its README. Corporate financial reporting remains under its dedicated reporting family within both the Russian and world corporate-disclosure routes; recurring official publications may route by source institution and stable publication series. An established domain route may group closely related source institutions when explicitly documented by the route.
5. Route primary publications by source under `scrolls/` and source-extracted observations by economic domain under `flakes/`. Do not add speculative categories or parallel country/publisher taxonomies in `flakes/`.
6. Keep paths and repository control files in English, lowercase, and ASCII where practical.
7. Routing directories, stable publication-series directories, and bridge families must contain a minimal README.md. Period/issue directories do not require one.
8. In `scrolls/`, keep natively machine-readable source artifacts such as HTML, XLSX, CSV, XML, and JSON in their original format. Source-extracted CSV observations belong to the source-specific `flakes/` series below. A narrowly scoped exception permits the Bank of Russia's monthly `DivisionsPerRegion_*.xlsx` releases under `flakes/banking/service-network/territorial-offices/` to be archived as documented, source-traceable `YYYY-MM-01_OFFICES-REG.csv` representations instead of preserved XLSX originals. This exception does not authorize inferred values, retrospective changes to source classifications, or claiming that a CSV is the original XLSX publication. A second narrowly scoped exception permits the Federal Tax Service's monthly regional SME registry statistics, verified against the publisher's JSON fields and Excel tables, to be kept as source-traceable dated CSV representations in `flakes/organizations/sme-register-population/`. The original JSON/XLSX is not archived in that series; one CSV per official date is updated in place on a subsequent material correction, with source hashes and retrieval details recorded in a publication event. A third narrowly scoped exception permits source-traceable **semantically named CSV representations** of the Federal Tax Service's Form 1-YUR regional legal-entity observations in `flakes/organizations/legal-entity-population/`, one current CSV per official reference date (not earlier than 2012-01-01). Keep only three published indicator constructs: non-ceased legal entities, entities undergoing bankruptcy proceedings when reported, and entities ceased through bankruptcy when reported. The `le_<construct>__<legal_kind>` columns are approved identity-only aliases for source fields as determined by the exact dated FNS taxonomy and indicator-code epoch; do not infer a single universal code dictionary. For older editions, exclude a source column when no reviewed direct reported-value equivalent to the current classification exists; absence is not zero. Never sum, subtract, calculate, or otherwise derive a canonical field within this exception. Preserve every source row, value of each retained observation, Russia and federal-district aggregates, composite territorial perimeters and their original row order. A single machine-readable `field-provenance.json` in the series describes each dated source-field-to-canonical-field identity mapping and omissions, retaining pre-conversion and post-conversion CSV hashes; the original source-coded CSV remains traceable in Git history. Source XLS/RAR/ZIP/OOXML bytes are not mirrored; publication events retain release/member identities and hashes and disclose source-internal inconsistencies. Historical taxonomy aliases must remain explicitly qualified by their source epoch and methodology, and a canonical name must never be presented as proof of economic comparability between editions. This exception authorizes no other transformations of FNS reports. Daily Bank of Russia bank-by-region HTML table observations are likewise retained only as dated source-extracted CSVs under `flakes/banking/service-network/bank-regional-offices/`. Do not extend these source-specific rules to other publishers. Store PDF publications in `scrolls/` as complete source-faithful Markdown transcriptions.
9. Keep all artifacts belonging to the same publication issue or release together. A single-artifact issue may remain a period/date-named file; create an issue directory when a release contains multiple related artifacts.
10. Add new top-level taxonomies only when a real source class requires them. Avoid speculative empty structure.
11. Do not infer the licence or legal status of a third-party artifact from its inclusion in the repository or from the root LICENSE. The root CC0 dedication applies only to original Tabularium contributions as defined in NOTICE.md. Preserve source provenance and do not assign CC0 or other Tabularium licensing metadata to third-party or source-derived content unless that legal status is explicitly established.



## Economic observation layer

- `flakes/` is routed by economic meaning, not country, publisher, format, or numeric-versus-semantic content type. Create only populated domains and stable series.
- A link to `scrolls/` is mandatory only when the actual underlying source document is preserved there; pin the revision if linked. Direct external captures with no archived source require no invented scroll reference, and external URLs and retrieval rules remain in collector code without a new universal source registry.
- Preserve reported values, source statuses, wording, units, period, geography, classification, row order, missing and zero. A common construct name is not proof of comparability. Never silently derive, adjust, normalize, fill or harmonize observations.
- Keep distinct the daily CBR capture date and official monthly reference date. FNS records have one current CSV per official date and no redundant latest copies.
- The original FNS publication event JSONL logs and Form 1-YUR `field-provenance.json` are immutable historical records. Resolve their old storage paths only on present-day file reads and links; do not rewrite history or rerun the completed 1-YUR semantic migration.
- Regenerable indexes can live in `registry/`, whereas methods, derivations, bridges and analytical claims are separate from the observation evidence layer.
