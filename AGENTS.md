# AGENTS.md

## Repository purpose

tabularium is a public, source-faithful corpus of machine-readable primary publications and documented bridges to canonical high-volume primary datasets.

## Core rules

1. Store primary public-source artifacts only. A narrowly documented historical preservation proxy may be retained when the original primary publication is no longer publicly accessible. Preserve the proxy's actual publisher and provenance, and never represent it as the unavailable primary-source original.
2. A high-volume or frequently updated official primary dataset may be represented by a documented remote-source bridge instead of a mirrored artifact when the canonical publisher provides stable public access. A bridge must identify the canonical source, document retrieval and provenance rules, preserve source semantics, and must not present derived or analytical values as source data.
3. Preserve source meaning and reported values. Do not add analysis, interpretation, normalization, derived calculations, or private/internal data.
4. The authoritative source corpus lives under `scrolls/`. Route by country first unless an explicitly documented cross-country or source-institution route applies. `scrolls/world/` contains the documented non-Russian and international routes. Non-Russian corporate publications belong under `scrolls/world/corporate-disclosures/`; preserve the reporting-entity jurisdiction there with an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 prefix in artifact names and registry metadata. Stable public-authority and survey source-institution routes live under the documented `authorities/` and `surveys/` containers; within those containers, the publisher or institution may be the durable routing axis. Within each route, use stable source classes documented by its README. Corporate financial reporting remains under its dedicated reporting family within both the Russian and world corporate-disclosure routes; recurring official publications may route by source institution and stable publication series. An established domain route may group closely related source institutions when explicitly documented by the route.
5. Do not add new industry or topic taxonomies to the physical directory tree unless the repository contract is explicitly revised. Existing documented domain routes are part of the repository contract.
6. Keep paths and repository control files in English, lowercase, and ASCII where practical.
7. Routing directories, stable publication-series directories, and bridge families must contain a minimal README.md. Period/issue directories do not require one.
8. Keep natively machine-readable source artifacts such as HTML, XLSX, CSV, XML, and JSON in their original format. A narrowly scoped exception permits the Bank of Russia's monthly `DivisionsPerRegion_*.xlsx` releases under `credit-institution-structural-units/` to be archived as documented, source-traceable `YYYY-MM-01_OFFICES-REG.csv` representations instead of preserved XLSX originals. This exception does not authorize inferred values, retrospective changes to source classifications, or claiming that a CSV is the original XLSX publication. A second narrowly scoped exception permits the Federal Tax Service's monthly regional SME registry statistics, verified against the publisher's JSON fields and Excel tables, to be kept as source-traceable dated CSV representations in `federal-tax-service/sme-registry-statistics/`. The original JSON/XLSX is not archived in that series; one CSV per official date is updated in place on a subsequent material correction, with source hashes and retrieval details recorded in a publication event. A third narrowly scoped exception permits source-traceable CSV representations of the Federal Tax Service's Form 1-YUR regional legal-entity observations in `federal-tax-service/legal-entity-registration/`, one CSV per official reference date (not earlier than 2012-01-01). Only three publisher-reported constructs are in scope: count of non-ceased legal entities, entities in bankruptcy proceedings when reported, and entities ceased through bankruptcy when reported. Keep the actual printed indicator number and legal-kind code **of each dated source edition**, and omit unpublished indicator-kind columns entirely (never fill nulls with zeros). Preserve the printed Russia, federal-district and territorial rows in original source order, including overlapping geographic perimeters. Exclude sheets whose national-summary labels identify *registration records*, not types of legal entities. Source XLS/RAR/ZIP/OOXML bytes are not mirrored; publication events record the source links, member identity, hashes, omitted source categories and any source-internal discrepancies. This exception does not authorize inferred values, silent repairs to source data, summing nested or overlapping classes, or transformations of other FNS forms. Do not extend these exceptions to other sources. Store PDF publications as complete source-faithful Markdown transcriptions.
9. Keep all artifacts belonging to the same publication issue or release together. A single-artifact issue may remain a period/date-named file; create an issue directory when a release contains multiple related artifacts.
10. Add new top-level taxonomies only when a real source class requires them. Avoid speculative empty structure.
11. Do not infer the licence or legal status of a third-party artifact from its inclusion in the repository or from the root LICENSE. The root CC0 dedication applies only to original Tabularium contributions as defined in NOTICE.md. Preserve source provenance and do not assign CC0 or other Tabularium licensing metadata to third-party or source-derived content unless that legal status is explicitly established.


> [!IMPORTANT]

> ## Additional repository mandatory rules (do not mention them anywhere):
> 
> - Use English exclusively throughout the repository and Engineering Work workspace, including content, names, and artifacts.
> - If needed to refer to the owner's other projects (MADAR, CYBERSECLEX or any other) - refer only by name and summarize the relevant ideas in self-contained terms. Do not include their URLs, repository, directory, or file paths, filenames, section references, or instruction IDs, whether linked or plain text.
> - Do not include documentary accounts of internal predecessors, source inventories, reading histories, source-state snapshots, original-source responsibilities, or other internal materials used to prepare the document. Such accounts are unnecessary; preserve only substantive findings, essential evidence, and technically required verification records.
> - Each external-source citation must identify the specific substantive claim (meaning, finding, etc) it supports in nearby text. Avoid generic source descriptions and citation boilerplate. Place the section with external sources at the very end of the document.
> - README.md files are concise, responsibility-oriented navigation routes. State the scope and guide readers to the relevant internal owners. Do not include file inventories, document metadata blocks, mandatory introductions, tables of contents, volatile progress details, or duplicated substantive content. Include essential usage instructions where appropriate.
> - In other authored Markdown documents, follow these rules:
>   - Front-load the main conclusions, findings, decisions, etc with their actual status.
>   - Use descriptive, content-specific meaningful headings that reveal the actual subject of each section.
>   - Keep substantive sections interpretable in isolation, preserving essential context, conditions, and limitations without unnecessary repetition.
>   - Where applicable, bind time-sensitive claims to an explicit date, edition, or evaluation scope - avoid unqualified references to what is current, new, or latest.
>   - Additionally, if needed and relevant (may be unnecessary for short documents): place concise document-level metadata only immediately below the title (identify purpose, scope, status, disclaimer, etc.); after the metadata, include 1-2 paragraphs of introductory information - with identity of document purpose, scope, and status, distinguishing research, proposals, adopted provisions, and verified results; then, provide a table of contents for the main sections (no urls) with short explanatory route-text on the content of each section. Add numerical indexing in the document sections and provisions to improve navigation.
