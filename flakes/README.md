# Flakes — source-extracted economic observations

`flakes/` owns source-faithful **algorithm-readable series artifacts** and assertions, routed by **economic meaning**, not country, publisher or file format. Each series can have a stable local extraction contract without a globally uniform layout. These are not necessarily normalized, mutually compatible, or analytically reconciled series.

## Routes

- [`organizations/`](organizations/) — populations and registries of organizational and entrepreneurial units.
- [`banking/`](banking/) — banking service infrastructure.

Selected untouched source files live in [`originals/`](../originals/); source-faithful AI-readable publications and source bridges live in [`scrolls/`](../scrolls/). Link a flake to either local layer only when the supporting artifact is actually retained there. Direct external captures need no invented archived-source link: retrieval rules remain in collector code.

Preserve literal published values, source identifiers, date, reporting perimeter, classification and missing-versus-zero distinctions. Derived results, cross-series bridges, adjustments and analytical claims are separate. Numeric and semantic records may share an economic route without automatically defining comparable constructs.
