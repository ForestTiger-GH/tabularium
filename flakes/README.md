# Flakes — source-extracted economic observations

`flakes/` owns source-faithful machine-readable observations and assertions, routed by **economic meaning**, not country, publisher or file format. These are not normalized or analytically reconciled series.

## Routes

- [`organizations/`](organizations/) — populations and registries of organizational and entrepreneurial units.
- [`banking/`](banking/) — banking service infrastructure.

Complete source publications and source bridges remain in [`scrolls/`](../scrolls/). Link a flake to a scroll only when its original source publication is actually archived there. Direct external captures without an archived scroll need no invented scroll reference: retrieval rules remain in collector code.

Preserve literal published values, source identifiers, date, reporting perimeter, classification and missing-versus-zero distinctions. Derived results, cross-series bridges, adjustments and analytical claims are separate. Numeric and semantic records may share an economic route without automatically defining comparable constructs.
