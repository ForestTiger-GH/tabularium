# Organizations

Source-extracted observations of legally registered organizational units and membership of special business registries.

## Routes

- [`legal-entity-population/`](legal-entity-population/) — official FNS Form 1-YUR registration observations.
- [`sme-register-population/`](sme-register-population/) — regional counts of legal entities and individual entrepreneurs in the FNS SME register.

These series have distinct population definitions. SME counts cannot be inferred by subtracting or filtering registered legal entities without an explicit analytical bridge.
