# Population of the Russian Federation by Municipalities

Native annual XLSX bulletins of Rosstat's `Численность населения Российской Федерации по муниципальным образованиям`.

## Route

Preserve each publisher workbook byte-for-byte, including all sheets, notes, classifications, and municipal-territory definitions. Use `YYYY.xlsx` for a workbook whose source states population estimates as of 1 January `YYYY`; a year in the filename denotes the population reference date, not necessarily the publication or retrieval date. A corrected workbook must remain distinguishable from the prior source version.

This series is archived as originals only; no obligatory `scrolls/` counterpart is created. A future `flakes/` series may extract algorithm-readable observations through its own documented, edition-aware contract. Do not silently equate municipality identifiers or territorial populations across different years or classifications.
