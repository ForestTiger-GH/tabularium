# Financial Reporting

Primary machine-readable general-purpose annual, interim, and quarterly financial reporting and closely related statutory or regulatory financial filings published by non-Russian entities and groups.

Use the artifact identity and filename contract documented in [../../README.md](../../README.md).

SEC filings used as financial-reporting representations, including forms such as 10-K, 10-Q, and 20-F, remain in this route and must not be duplicated under a regulator-specific directory.

Preserve the reporting entity or group perimeter, accounting basis, filing regime, publication variant, period, units, and source wording as source identity. Do not infer equivalence between different accounting frameworks, filing variants, legal entities, or consolidated groups.

Keep natively machine-readable source artifacts in their original format and represent PDF publications as complete source-faithful Markdown transcriptions according to the repository rules.
