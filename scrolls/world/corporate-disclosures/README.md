# Corporate Disclosures

Primary machine-readable corporate publications from reporting entities outside the Russian Federation.

## Route

- [financial-reporting/](financial-reporting/) — general-purpose annual, interim, and quarterly financial reporting and closely related statutory or regulatory filings whose principal evidentiary value is the financial reporting representation.
- [annual-reports/](annual-reports/) — broad annual or integrated corporate reports published as a distinct primary publication class.
- [strategies/](strategies/) — standalone corporate strategy, development-plan, guidance, outlook, and forecast publications grouped by publication year.

Use the artifact identity and filename contract documented in [../README.md](../README.md).

SEC filings used as financial-reporting representations, including forms such as 10-K, 10-Q, and 20-F, belong in `financial-reporting/`. Do not create or use a parallel regulator-specific copy of the same filing.

Keep one primary material physically once. A document already stored as an annual report or financial-reporting artifact is not duplicated in `strategies/` merely because it contains strategic targets or forward-looking statements. Preserve reporting entity, jurisdiction, reporting perimeter, period or publication date as applicable, accounting framework where relevant, filing or publication class, source format, version, and provenance as supported by the source.
