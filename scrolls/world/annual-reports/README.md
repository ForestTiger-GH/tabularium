# Annual Reports

Primary machine-readable annual and integrated corporate reports published by non-Russian entities and groups as distinct primary publications.

Use the artifact identity and filename contract documented in [../README.md](../README.md).

An annual report remains an annual-report source even when it contains audited financial statements. A separately published financial statement, filing, or other primary representation belongs in the appropriate source class and must not be silently treated as identical to the annual report.

Keep each publication source-faithful and preserve its actual reporting perimeter, period, version, and provenance.
