# Federal Tax Service

Primary statistical publications of Russia's Federal Tax Service.

## Route

- [SME registry statistics](sme-registry-statistics/) — monthly regional counts of legal entities and individual entrepreneurs in the official SME register, preserved as source-traceable CSV snapshots.
- [Legal-entity registration, Form 1-YUR](legal-entity-registration/) — dated regional source-traceable extracts of the FNS registered-legal-entity statistics (reported indicators 1, 8, 12).

Each release is identified by its official statistical reference date, not by its retrieval date.
