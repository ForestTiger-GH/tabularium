# Banking Sector Dynamics

Source-faithful Markdown transcriptions of the recurring Bank of Russia information-analytical publication `О развитии банковского сектора Российской Федерации`.

Official publication series: https://www.cbr.ru/analytics/bank_sector/develop/

## Route

Keep monthly issues directly under this series without calendar-year partitions. Identify each issue by its **reference month**, not its later publication month; preserve the source-related filename pattern `razv_bs_YY_MM.md` when applicable.

A single-file issue may remain at this level. When an issue includes accompanying figures or other artifacts, keep the Markdown and all companion files together in a `YYYY-MM/` issue directory, preserving their relative links.

Retain the reported values, tables, notes, methodology, data-cutoff dates, and publication-specific revisions as stated in each release. Do not silently combine or overwrite observations from different releases.
