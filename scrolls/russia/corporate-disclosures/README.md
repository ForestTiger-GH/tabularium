# Corporate Disclosures

Primary machine-readable corporate disclosures published by Russian corporate entities and groups, plus documented bridges to canonical regulatory reporting datasets for banks.

## Route

- [financial-reporting/](financial-reporting/) — IFRS, Russian Accounting Standards reporting, and Bank of Russia regulatory bank-reporting bridges.
- [annual-reports/](annual-reports/) — annual reports.
- [issuer-reports/](issuer-reports/) — periodic issuer reports.

Store each source document as a complete source-faithful representation according to the repository rules. Keep distinct publication classes in their documented routes. Remote bridge routes do not mirror high-volume source datasets unless the repository contract is explicitly changed.
