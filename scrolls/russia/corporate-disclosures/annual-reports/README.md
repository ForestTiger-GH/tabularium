# Annual Reports

Machine-readable annual reports published by Russian legal entities.

## Route

Store each annual-report source artifact as a complete source-faithful representation in this series.

When the source explicitly identifies an appendix or data book as part of an annual report, keep it in this series alongside the main report and preserve it as a distinct source artifact rather than merging their contents.

Annual reports remain distinct from standalone financial statements even when they contain or reproduce financial reporting.
