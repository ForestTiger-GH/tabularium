# Surveys

Admitted analytical, research, statistical, and market-survey publication routes for the Russian corpus.

## Route

- [`dom-rf-analytics/`](dom-rf-analytics/) — recurring analytical publications issued by DOM.RF Analytics.
- [`moex-exchange/`](moex-exchange/) — recurring publications from Moscow Exchange.
- [`spb-exchange/`](spb-exchange/) — recurring SPB Exchange trading-results materials.

## Exchange publication series

| Exchange | Series | Frequency / update model | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moscow Exchange | Index reviews | Quarterly | Official Moscow Exchange website |
| Moscow Exchange | Trading volumes | Cumulative historical dataset; updated periodically | Official Moscow Exchange website |
| Moscow Exchange | Bond secondary trading | Monthly | Official Moscow Exchange website |
| Moscow Exchange | Trading results | Monthly; annual summary releases may also occur | Official Moscow Exchange website |
| Moscow Exchange | Retail investor activity | Monthly; annual summary releases may also occur | Official Moscow Exchange website |
| SPB Exchange | Trading results | Monthly; annual summary releases also occur | Official SPB Exchange where available; documented third-party preservation proxies for unavailable historical originals |

Preserve publisher identity, publication-series identity, source format, provenance, and the distinction between primary publications and explicitly documented preservation proxies.
