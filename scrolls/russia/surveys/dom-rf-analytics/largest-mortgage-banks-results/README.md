# Largest Mortgage Banks Results

Machine-readable recurring reports by DOM.RF Analytics on the results of the largest mortgage banks.

Frequency: monthly.

## Route

Partition monthly issues by calendar year of the reporting period. Store each issue as a complete source-faithful Markdown representation and keep the reporting period stated by the source distinct from the publication date.

Use `YYYY-MM.md` for monthly issue filenames, keyed to the reporting period stated by the source.
